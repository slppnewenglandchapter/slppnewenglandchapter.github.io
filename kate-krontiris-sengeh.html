About the Speaker

Creativity, Leadership and Civic Transformation

A distinguished voice at the intersection of culture, organizational development and public participation.

Kate Krontiris Sengeh

Kate Krontiris Sengeh is a cultural strategist, facilitator, artist, and organizational development practitioner whose work connects creativity, civic participation, leadership, and community transformation. Through initiatives supporting artists, institutions, and cultural development, she has contributed to strengthening Sierra Leone’s creative and civic landscape.

Read more

Her work brings together artists, policymakers, organizations, funders, and communities to build stronger cultural ecosystems and more participatory institutions. She is also associated with initiatives advancing Sierra Leone’s creative industries and civic spaces.
