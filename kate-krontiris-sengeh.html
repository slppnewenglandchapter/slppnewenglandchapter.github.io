About the Speaker

Creativity, Leadership and Civic Transformation

A distinguished voice at the intersection of culture, organizational development and public participation.

Kate Krontiris Sengeh

Kate Krontiris Sengeh is a cultural strategist, facilitator, artist, and organizational development practitioner whose work connects creativity, civic participation, leadership, and community transformation. Through initiatives supporting artists, institutions, and cultural development, she has contributed to strengthening Sierra Leone’s creative and civic landscape.

Read more

Her work brings together artists, policymakers, organizations, funders, and communities to build stronger cultural ecosystems and more participatory institutions. She is also associated with initiatives advancing Sierra Leone’s creative industries and civic spaces.

01

Culture & National Identity

How creativity can strengthen belonging, community, memory, and sustainable national development.

Tap to learn moreTap to close⌄

Kate founded Studio Culture, a strategy, policy, and artmaking studio that helps translate cultural ideas into the language of strategy, investment, and development. She also co-founded SoundWave Creative, which works to strengthen and advance Sierra Leone’s music sector. Her work demonstrates how culture and creativity can support national development while preserving identity and artistic expression.

Explore Kate’s cultural work →
02

Leadership & Civic Participation

Building institutions that listen, include, empower, and invite citizens into meaningful public life.

Tap to learn moreTap to close⌄

Kate serves as Co-Chair of the Miatta Civic Centre Advisory Council, supporting the transformation of a historic national building into an inclusive center for civic expression, cultural celebration, creative innovation, and digital advancement. The initiative is designed to bring citizens, artists, innovators, institutions, and government together to speak, create, connect, and help shape public life.

03

Diaspora Engagement

Connecting global experience, professional networks, and community service with Sierra Leone’s progress.

Based in Sierra Leone and engaged internationally, Kate’s work connects artists, policymakers, funders, institutions, and the public across geographic and professional boundaries. Through cultural strategy, facilitation, music, and institution-building, she helps translate global experience and partnerships into locally rooted creative and civic development.
