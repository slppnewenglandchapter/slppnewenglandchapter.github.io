About Kate

Culture, civic life and collaborative leadership

A distinctive voice at the intersection of culture, civic life, creativity and collaborative leadership.

Kate Krontiris Sengeh is a cultural strategist, facilitator, artist and organizational development practitioner. Her work brings together communities, institutions, artists and leaders to strengthen civic participation, creative systems and inclusive public life.

At this inauguration, her experience offers a practical lens on how chapter leaders can build belonging, invite participation and turn shared purpose into meaningful service.

Prelude to the Special Guest Address

Three ideas that set the stage for Kate’s conversation

Before Kate takes the stage, these themes introduce the experience and perspective she brings to the Executive Inauguration. Together, they connect her work to the chapter’s new leadership mandate, the meaning of service, and the wider purpose of the celebration.

01

Culture & National Identity

How creativity can strengthen belonging, community memory and sustainable national development.

Kate founded Studio Culture, a strategy, policy and artmaking practice that helps translate cultural ideas into the language of strategy, investment and development. She also co-founded SoundWave Creative, which works to strengthen the systems and opportunities supporting Sierra Leone’s music and creative sectors.

Connection to the Inauguration
This theme invites the incoming executive and the wider community to consider how culture can deepen belonging, strengthen chapter identity and turn shared heritage into purposeful service.

02

Leadership & Civic Participation

Building institutions that listen, include, empower and invite citizens into meaningful public life.

As Co-Chair of the Miatta Civic Centre Advisory Council, Kate contributes to the renewal of a historic national institution as an inclusive space for civic expression, cultural celebration, creative innovation and public participation.

Connection to the Inauguration
The swearing-in of a new executive is not only ceremonial; it is a public commitment to listen, include, organize and create meaningful opportunities for members to participate.

03

Diaspora Engagement & Collaborative Leadership

Connecting global experience, professional networks and community service with locally rooted progress.

Through cultural strategy, facilitation, music and institution-building, Kate brings together artists, policymakers, organizations, funders and communities across geographic and professional boundaries. Her work demonstrates how dialogue and collaborative leadership can turn diverse experience into shared action.

Connection to the Inauguration
A chapter serving a diverse diaspora must connect people across generations, professions and locations while building trust around a shared agenda. This theme prepares the audience for that broader leadership conversation.

Together, these themes serve as a prelude to the special guest address. They introduce the leadership questions behind Kate’s appearance without attempting to pre-empt the speech itself. Related initiatives and official website links appear once in the Selected Work section above.
