About the Minister

Governance, gender equity and national development

A trailblazing voice at the intersection of legislative courage, gender equity and Sierra Leone's national development agenda.

Hon. Madam Manty Tarawalli, MSc serves as Minister of State, Office of the Vice President, and Principal Assistant to Vice President Dr. Mohamed Juldeh Jalloh, where she coordinates several of Sierra Leone's most consequential multi-sectoral development programs. A graduate of the London School of Economics and the University of Salford, she is internationally recognized for her leadership in governance, gender equality and sustainable development.

As former Minister of Gender and Children's Affairs, she led the multi-year campaign that culminated in the passage of the landmark Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment (GEWE) Act, 2022 — legislation reserving 30 percent of public and private sector positions for women, extending maternity leave to 14 weeks, and mandating equal pay for equal work. Her leadership has since been recognized on the world stage, including the Harvard Ministerial Leadership Forum's Minister of the Year award.

Prelude to the Keynote Address

Three ideas that set the stage for the Minister's keynote

Before Minister Tarawalli takes the stage, these themes introduce the record and perspective she brings to the Executive Inauguration. Together, they connect her leadership to the chapter's new mandate, the meaning of service, and the wider purpose of the celebration.

01

Legislative Courage & Gender Equity

How sustained advocacy, coalition-building and political will can turn a stalled bill into transformative national law.

As Minister of Gender and Children's Affairs, Minister Tarawalli spent years building support for the GEWE Act after it was initially resisted within Parliament and her own party. Her strategy, sharpened through the Harvard Ministerial Leadership Program, combined civil-society partnership with disciplined legislative sequencing to secure passage.

Connection to the Inauguration
A newly sworn-in executive inherits the same task: converting good intentions into structures—bylaws, committees, budgets—that outlast any single term.

02

Multi-Sectoral National Development

Coordinating institutions, ministries and international partners toward a single national development agenda.

In the Office of the Vice President, Minister Tarawalli supervises the Millennium Challenge Compact, Scaling Up Nutrition and the Sierra Leone Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative—inter-ministerial programs that require constant coordination across government, donors and civil society.

Connection to the Inauguration
Chapter leadership across committees and sister chapters demands the same coordination—aligning many hands toward one shared purpose.

03

Global Leadership & Diaspora Recognition

Representing Sierra Leone on the world stage while remaining accountable to citizens at home.

Recognized as Harvard's Minister of the Year and nominated for the Women Changing the World Awards, Minister Tarawalli has carried Sierra Leone's development story to international platforms while continuing to answer for results at home.

Connection to the Inauguration
A diaspora chapter lives in that same tension—representing Sierra Leone abroad while remaining accountable to its members and to home.

Together, these themes serve as a prelude to the keynote address. They introduce the leadership questions behind the Minister's appearance without attempting to pre-empt the speech itself. Related sources and official coverage appear once in the Selected Work section above.
