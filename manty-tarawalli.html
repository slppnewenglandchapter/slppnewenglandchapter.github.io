About the Minister

Leadership with a record behind it

Hon. Madam Manty Tarawalli serves as Minister of State in the Office of the Vice President of Sierra Leone, bringing experience across governance, gender equality and multi-sectoral national development.

As former Minister of Gender and Children's Affairs, she played a central leadership role in the multi-year effort that culminated in passage of Sierra Leone's Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act, 2022. Her work on the reform was later recognized by Harvard's Ministerial Leadership Program with the Harvard Ministerial Medal of Achievement.

Her public-service portfolio has also included national coordination work involving food systems, extractive-industry transparency and accountability. That combination of legislation, institutional coordination and public leadership gives her keynote particular relevance to a chapter entering a new executive term.

The Record Behind the Keynote

Four dimensions of leadership

A concise view of the work and recognition that frame Minister Tarawalli's appearance in New England.

Gender Equality

Leadership behind the GEWE Act

Her tenure as Minister of Gender and Children's Affairs included sustained advocacy, consultation and political strategy supporting passage of Sierra Leone's landmark Gender Equality and Women's Empowerment Act, 2022.

Recognition

Harvard Ministerial Medal of Achievement

Harvard's Ministerial Leadership Program recognized her contribution to landmark gender-equity legislation with its Ministerial Medal of Achievement.

Transparency

Extractive-industry accountability

Her public-service work has included leadership within Sierra Leone's extractive-industry transparency framework, bringing government, civil society and industry into one accountability structure.

National Development

Cross-sector coordination

From the Office of the Vice President, her work has intersected with national food systems, public-sector reform and other cross-government priorities requiring coordination beyond a single ministry.

Why it matters here: A newly inaugurated chapter executive faces the same core challenge in a different setting: turn vision into structure, coordinate people across roles and sustain public trust through service.
Why Her Voice Matters

A keynote suited to a moment of transition

This inauguration is not only ceremonial. It marks the beginning of a new chapter mandate and a public commitment to lead.

  • From advocacy to institutions: turning priorities into durable systems.
  • From individual roles to shared purpose: coordinating leaders, committees and communities.
  • From visibility to accountability: representing an organization with credibility at home and across the diaspora.

Minister Tarawalli's record gives those questions practical weight.
